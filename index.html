Interests & motivations

Language Learning

To learn another language is to acquire another soul!

I have been learning French for 7+ years, and I have noticed that there is a need / demand for better language learning platforms.

Often we find that online or "fun" platforms trade depth and complexity for simplicity, meaning higher level learners such as B2,C1,C2 often struggle to find resources to help them outside of expensive textbooks and tutors.

I am actively working on a side project to solve some of these issues, by using my own learning journey as the benchmark for how platforms should operate.
